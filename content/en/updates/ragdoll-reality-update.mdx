## Halloween Update Breakdown: How the Zombie Graveyard Works

The loop is simple to explain and surprisingly hard to master. Grab a dummy, haul it to the graveyard, and throw it in. It rises as a zombie. From there, the encounter escalates based on which tier you spawned and how many other dummies are nearby.

- **Slow zombies** shamble toward you and are easy to outpace, which makes them ideal for farming kills and setting up combos.
- **Normal zombies** keep a steady chase and bite when they close the gap, so they punish players who stand still.
- **Fast zombies** sprint and pounce. They are the ones that end runs, and player experience suggests they should always be your first target.

The infection mechanic is what makes this more than a simple wave mode. When a zombie bites a dummy, that dummy turns too. Drop five dummies near the graveyard and you can accidentally build a horde in under a minute.

| Zombie Type | Behavior | Best Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Slow | Shambles forward, low pressure | Melee farming, hazard bait |
| Normal | Steady chase, bites at close range | Push and Grab to reset distance |
| Fast | Sprints and pounces | Sniper, Shotgun, or a hazard funnel |
| Infected (any tier) | Created when a zombie bites a dummy | Contain early before it snowballs |

### Practical Tips for Surviving the Horde

1. **Never fight in the open.** Back into a chokepoint near the shredder or spike pit so only one zombie reaches you at a time.
2. **Convert deliberately.** Drop one dummy in the graveyard at a time rather than dumping a pile and losing control of the arena.
3. **Kill fast zombies at range.** A Sniper or Shotgun pick before they close saves you a healing-free death.
4. **Use Push, not fists, to open fights.** Shoving a zombie into a hazard costs nothing and removes a body from the board.
5. **Keep a fallback route.** The 80-stud tower is not just a kill tool — it is also somewhere to reset the chase.

## Weapons in the Ragdoll Reality Update: What to Unlock First

Kills unlock new weapons, which means your early progression should be deliberate. Fists and Push are free, so treat them as tools rather than damage dealers — Push exists to move bodies into hazards, and Grab exists to move bodies into the graveyard.

| Weapon | Role | Best Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| Fists | Melee | Finishing stumbling dummies |
| Push | Utility | Shoving targets into hazards |
| Grab | Utility | Hauling dummies to the graveyard or tower |
| Pistol | Ranged | Reliable limb shots at mid range |
| Bat | Melee | Knockback toward ledges and pits |
| Machete | Melee | Limb removal in close quarters |
| Shotgun | Close ranged | Clearing a cluster of normal zombies |
| Sniper | Long ranged | Deleting fast zombies before they pounce |
| Minigun | Sustained fire | Horde suppression when you are cornered |
| Rocket Launcher | Explosive | Group damage on stacked targets |
| Flamethrower | Area denial | Holding a chokepoint |
| C4 | Trap | Pre-placing on a zombie path |

A sensible unlock order is Shotgun first for crowd control, then Sniper for fast-zombie picks, then Minigun or Rocket Launcher once hordes get thick. Flamethrower and C4 are luxury picks — devastating in the right setup, awkward when you are being chased.

### Three Loadout Rules That Actually Help

- **Pair one utility with one damage weapon.** Grab plus Shotgun beats two damage weapons almost every time.
- **Match range to your arena.** Fighting near the tower favors melee and Push; fighting near the train tracks favors ranged.
- **Rotate after each horde.** The **Ragdoll Reality update** punishes players who stand still with an empty magazine.

## Deadly Playground: Hazard Combos Worth Learning

The environment is the strongest weapon in the game, and it is free. Every hazard on the map interacts with the ragdoll physics, so the real skill ceiling is learning which one to use on which target.

| Hazard | Effect | Strongest Combo |
|---|---|---|
| Shredder | Instant destruction | Push zombies in from behind |
| Train | High-speed impact along the track | Lure fast zombies onto the rails |
| Wrecking Ball | Swinging blunt force | Knock targets into its arc |
| Spike Pit | Fall and impact damage | Grab and throw from the edge |
| Human Cannon | Long-distance launch | Fire dummies into the graveyard |
| Seesaw | Physics-based launcher | Drop a heavy dummy on one end |
| 80-Stud Tower | Massive fall damage | Throw or shove from the top |

The Human Cannon into the graveyard is the sleeper combo of this update — it converts a dummy into a zombie at range, which means you can build a horde without walking into it. Community reports also favor the seesaw for launching bodies into the shredder, though it takes timing to land consistently.
