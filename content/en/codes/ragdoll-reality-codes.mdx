## What to Practice Instead: Dummies and Weapon Control

No codes today doesn't mean a wasted session. The game rewards deliberate testing more than most sandbox titles, and a structured first hour builds skills that carry into every mode. Start with one dummy, one weapon, and a familiar distance — then change exactly one variable per trial.

### Read the reaction, not the fall

Location-based damage is the core system. According to the game's official description, leg impacts can leave a dummy limping or crawling, arm impacts can leave that arm hanging while the dummy clutches the wound, and heavy hits can cause staggering, bleeding, or limb loss. Predict the reaction before you swing, then compare what actually happened.

| Target area | Expected reaction | What a beginner should watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leg | Limp, crawl, loss of balance | Movement of the struck leg after impact |
| Arm | Arm hangs, target protects the wound | Whether the dummy guards that side |
| Torso | Stagger or backward shift | Use as a baseline for heavier hits |
| Head | Strong full-body reaction | Keep it separate from body tests |
| Detached limb | Bleeding, loss of attachment | Pause and observe before swinging again |

If your dummy steps away, raises its arms, or changes direction mid-test, reset instead of chasing. A controlled second attempt teaches you more than ten chaotic swings.

### Test weapons with a fixed target

The official material doesn't publish a damage ranking, so player experience is your best comparison tool — but only if you record the variables. Test four representative tools: one familiar, one fast, one heavy, one ranged. Rate each from 1 to 5.

| Rating | Question to ask | Passing standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Control | Can I hit the intended area twice in a row? | Two clean hits |
| Consistency | Does the third hit match the first? | Similar reaction |
| Recovery | How fast can I reset for the next target? | Under a few seconds |
| Clarity | Can I tell which hit caused the result? | Yes, without guessing |

### Two drills worth running

- **Controlled target cycle:** left leg, right leg, left arm, torso — one clean swing each. Complete the cycle before increasing distance. If a segment misses, repeat it instead of moving on.
- **Speed ladder:** stage one is slow and deliberate with three intended targets hit. Stage two is moderate and consistent while moving, for two clean cycles. Don't advance until the prior stage is boring.
